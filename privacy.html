Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026. The short version: your documents never leave your iPhone, and we never see them.

What Kip stores

Kip stores the documents you type in: names, numbers, dates and the other fields you add. Everything is kept in an encrypted database on your iPhone. The encryption key is created on the device and protected by the iOS Keychain, by Face ID or by the passcode you set in Kip. The developer has no copy of your documents and no way to read them.

What Kip does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no servers of our own.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • No backup or sync: Kip never sends your documents anywhere, including to us.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Face ID and passcode

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. Kip only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data. The Kip passcode is used to wrap the encryption key on the device and is not stored in plain form.

Purchases

Kip Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your purchase history under Apple's privacy policy. Kip receives only the status of your subscription from the App Store on your device.

Clipboard

When you copy a value, Kip puts it on the iPhone clipboard marked local-only and expiring after 60 seconds. Any app you paste into after that receives the value as with any copy.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything removes every document, profile and setting from Kip. Deleting the app does the same. Documents you delete one by one wait in Recently deleted for 30 days, then disappear.

Children

Kip is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date. Questions: feedback@kip-docs.com.
