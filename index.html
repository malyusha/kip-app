Any document in two taps

Kip keeps passports, IDs, licences and cards on your iPhone, encrypted, and copies a number with one tap.

Contact

Questions, problems, ideas: feedback@kip-docs.com. We answer within a few days.

How Kip works

  • Adding a document. Tap +, pick a type (passport, driving licence, insurance card…) or start a blank card, type the fields in. Kip never needs a photo.
  • Copying a number. Open the document and tap a field. The value goes to the clipboard for 60 seconds, then clears itself.
  • Secret fields. PINs and similar values are hidden with dots. Tap the eye to reveal one for 30 seconds.
  • Locking Kip. Settings → Security: Face ID, a six-digit passcode, and how soon Kip locks after you leave. Kip also locks when the screen turns off.
  • Grouping. The switch at the start of the chip row groups documents by their canvas. Hold a tile to move it, swipe left to delete it (Undo for a few seconds, then Recently deleted for 30 days).

Kip Plus

  • Free Kip keeps up to 8 documents in one profile. Everything you saved stays open and copyable at the limit.
  • Kip Plus is an auto-renewable subscription (yearly or monthly) sold through the App Store. It lifts the limit and adds profiles for family, pets and the car.
  • Restore purchases: Settings → Kip Plus → Restore purchases, or open the paywall and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.
  • Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kip Plus. Your documents stay after Plus ends.

Your data

  • Documents live only on this iPhone, encrypted. Kip has no account, no cloud and no analytics. See the privacy policy.
  • Deleting everything: Settings → Erase everything, or delete the app.
  • Kip does not back up or sync your documents. Keep your originals.
